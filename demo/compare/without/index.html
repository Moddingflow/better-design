    Three ways to spend the night

    Every ticket includes a pillow, blanket and water. Couchettes and cabins add bedding and breakfast.

    Seat

    from €39

    Reclining seat in a six-seat compartment. Lights dim after midnight.

    • Reading light & socket
    • Luggage rack overhead
    • Shared washroom

    Couchette

    from €69

    A flat berth in a compartment of four or six, with sheets and a lockable door.

    • Bedding made up on arrival
    • Women-only compartments
    • Breakfast included

    Private cabin

    from €149

    A room for one to three people with real beds, a washbasin and a key card.

    • Deluxe cabins with shower & WC
    • Hot breakfast served to the cabin
    • Welcome drink & slippers

    City centre to city centre

    No airport transfers or security queues. Board up to 30 minutes before departure.

    Bikes and big bags

    Bicycle spaces on every train, bookable as an add-on. No baggage weight limits.

    Change of plans

    Free changes until 24 hours before departure on all fares, including seats.
